Walking Routes to School

Baltimore City · built entirely from public Open Baltimore, BCDOT & MTA data. Modeled on Minneapolis’ “Walking Routes for Youth” map.

How routes are scored: every route is checked against Baltimore’s High Injury Network, the half-mile street segments with the most pedestrian crashes and serious injuries or deaths, 2019–2023. “Caution” means the route runs along or within ~20 m of one of these segments. It does not model sidewalks or crossing conditions.

Sources: BCPSS school locations, Baltimore City Recreation & Parks (parks, rec centers), Enoch Pratt Free Library, MTA bus stops, BCDOT traffic signals, Baltimore City High Injury Network (via Open Baltimore / ArcGIS). Sidewalks and crosswalks are loaded live from OpenStreetMap contributors (the regional 2021 sidewalk/crosswalk layers from the Baltimore Metropolitan Council are not publicly downloadable).
